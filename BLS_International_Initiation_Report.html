1. Company Overview & Business Architecture

Founded in 2005 and listed on the Indian bourses in 2016, BLS International Services Limited is a technology-enabled outsourcing partner for sovereign governments and diplomatic missions worldwide. The company manages non-discretionary, administrative tasks related to visa applications, passport issuance, consular services, citizen ID enrollment, and document attestation. Operating across 65+ countries for 46+ client governments, BLS has processed over 500 million applications since inception.

1.1 Engine 1: Visa & Consular Services (High-Margin Value Engine)

• FY26 Revenue: ₹1,840 Crore (+11.3% YoY) | EBITDA Margin: 40.1% (₹738 Cr EBITDA).
• Concession Model: Wins 3-to-5 year exclusive sovereign tenders → deploys physical Visa Application Centres (VACs) → collects statutory consular fee + applicant service fee.
• The Spread Concept: BLS reports "Net Revenue" (Gross Revenue minus direct passport pass-through costs). Margin expansion is driven by high-ticket Value-Added Services (VAS: biometric capture, door-to-door courier, VIP premium lounges, translation, and travel insurance).
• Key Sovereigns: Spain (global contract covering 58+ countries), Germany, Italy, Hungary, Canada, and various Schengen states.

1.2 Engine 2: Digital Services via BLS E-Services (Listed 51% Subsidiary)

• FY26 Revenue: ₹1,158 Crore (+114.4% YoY) | EBITDA Margin: 7.0% (₹81 Cr EBITDA).
• Financial Inclusion Network: Operates as a Master Business Correspondent (BC) and Business Facilitator (BF) for State Bank of India (SBI) and other major public sector lenders, managing 150,000+ merchant touchpoints.
• Strategic Acquisitions: Scaled through acquisitions of Aadifidelis Solutions (loan distribution), Starfin India, and Zero Mass.
• Public Listing: BLS E-Services (NSE: BLSE) was spun out and listed in February 2024, commanding a standalone market capitalization of ₹2,934 Crore.
