The Core Thesis

BLS International is the sole publicly listed pure-play visa outsourcing firm globally (~15% market share alongside private peers VFS Global and TLScontact). The market over-penalized the stock for the temporary MEA debarment (quashed by Delhi HC) and UAE consular re-tendering (~12% revenue exposure).

Financial Characteristics

Consolidated margins (27.3%) are optically diluted by the 51% Digital Services subsidiary (BLSE, 7.0% EBITDA). Core Visa & Consular operations deliver 40.1% EBITDA margins (₹738 Cr), ₹3,302 Net Rev/App, 29.8% ROCE, 32.1% ROE, and ₹1,562 Cr in net cash (₹1,617 Cr gross cash less ₹55 Cr debt).
